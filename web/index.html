Try the native version on your iPhone ＋
  1. Download the code on a Mac with Xcode.
  2. Open VoiceOrbPlayground.xcodeproj.
  3. In Signing & Capabilities, select your development team and use a unique bundle identifier.
  4. Connect your iPhone, enable Developer Mode if prompted, select it in Xcode and press Run.

The download is source code, not a one-tap iPhone installer. You can also open this web preview directly on your phone.
